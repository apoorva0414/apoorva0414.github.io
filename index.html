    The long ridge

    Five years at Oracle's Applications Lab. I took requirements from business teams and turned them into workflows people actually used. I program-managed eight quarterly upgrades, and was the scrum master when the team needed one.

    Into the backpack: translating between engineers and the business

    Rain2024

    Then it rained.

    A restructure landed me on a project with no clear brief, no requirements and no support. And in global strategy meetings, I was the most technical person in the room and the least able to shape the conversation.

    The problem was never the code. Nobody had decided what we needed, and I wanted to be the one who does.

    I didn't stay for the sequel.
